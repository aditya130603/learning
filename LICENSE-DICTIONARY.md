# Dictionary licensing and provenance

Word Mayhem's optional offline dictionary is generated from **Open English WordNet 2025** by the Open English Wordnet Community, derived from Princeton WordNet. Open English WordNet is distributed under **CC BY 4.0** and requires attribution to the Open English Wordnet team and Princeton WordNet. See https://en-word.net/ and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ and https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use.

The dictionary build preserves the source glosses and synonyms, though it caps the number of senses and example sentences per entry to reduce package size. This is not an exhaustive dictionary of all English words, nor is every definition suitable for young children. Only adult search has access to these entries. Curated child courses are separate. The built-in grammar model is also separate.
